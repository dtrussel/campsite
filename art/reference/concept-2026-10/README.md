# Concept art, October 2026

These are the team's concept paintings: 12 images, supplied as PNG and stored here as WebP at quality 90.
They are **reference only**. The game never loads them.

The style guide built from them is in
[`docs/design/art-direction.md`](../../../docs/design/art-direction.md).
The work that applies them is
[`.features/031-concept-look-pass/`](../../../.features/031-concept-look-pass/plan.md).

| File | Shows | Canonical for |
|------|-------|---------------|
| `01_campsite_daytime_overview.webp` | The whole camp from a high tilt-shift view in golden-hour light | **Target look:** lighting, grade, camera, density, camp layout |
| `02_campsite_campfire_close_view.webp` | A closer view of the fire, tent, stall and crates | Camp props: tent, stone ring, log and stump seats, tripod pot, signpost, lanterns, crates |
| `03_campsite_night_mood.webp` | The same camp at night | **Night look:** deep blue world, warm pools of fire and lantern light, moon, glowing tent |
| `04_leo_character_turnaround.webp` | Leo turnaround | Confirms the current Leo (navy cap, blue tee, teal shorts, bedroll pack, compass) |
| `05_nela_character_turnaround.webp` | Nela turnaround | Confirms the current Nela (ponytail, white tee, purple folk-pattern pants, bunny on her pack) |
| `06_bramble_beast_concept.webp` | A rock-and-log golem with a flower crown | **Not adopted.** The game keeps the feature 026 design |
| `07_mushroom_goblin_concept.webp` | A green goblin with a red spotted cap | **Not adopted.** The game keeps the feature 027 design |
| `08_shadow_imp_concept.webp` | A violet-black imp in purple smoke | **Not adopted.** The game keeps the feature 028 design. The smoke aura may be borrowed for VFX |
| `09_forest_path_environment.webp` | A dirt path, green pines, wildflowers and a signpost | Ground, path and flower dressing |
| `10_river_bridge_environment.webp` | A waterfall, a river and a plank bridge | Future (out of scope for 031) |
| `11_ancient_ruins_environment.webp` | Mossy stone ruins | Future biome (out of scope) |
| `12_forest_village_environment.webp` | Cottages, a well and a market stall | Well and stall props; houses are future scope |

Sheets 04–12 are crops from a contact sheet, so they are small and soft.
Use them for shapes and colours, not detail.
