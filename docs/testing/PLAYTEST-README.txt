CAMPSITE - Playtest build 0.24.0 (playtest 1)
============================================

Thanks for testing! This is an early prototype. Turn your sound on:
an owl calls when night is coming. Music and sound volume are in the
pause menu (Esc). The game needs a reasonably recent graphics driver.

HOW TO START
------------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. your Desktop).
   If you got TWO zips (part1-program and part2-data), unzip both
   into the SAME folder, so Campsite.exe and Campsite.pck sit side
   by side.
2. Double-click Campsite.exe.
3. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the game is
   not signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. Pick a run: 3 NIGHTS (short) or 7 NIGHTS (long). A picture help
   screen shows the controls; click "Go!". Press H or the ? button to
   see it again.
5. The game saves by itself every morning. Next time, CONTINUE on the
   title screen carries on from that morning.

GOAL
----
Survive all the nights. Keep the campfire burning!
- During the DAY, gather resources, build defences and craft.
- At NIGHT, monsters come out of the forest:
    Shadow Imps ...... go for the campfire, and for you if you get close
    Bramble Beasts ... big and slow; they smash buildings first. Watch
                       for the fists going up: step away before the
                       SLAM lands!
    Mushroom Gremlins  fast thieves; they grab resources from your
                       stash and run. Catch them to get the loot back.
- You lose if the campfire goes out or if Leo is knocked out.

CONTROLS (like League of Legends)
--------
  Right click .......... move / attack a monster / gather /
                         use the campfire / repair a damaged building
  Left click ........... attack or use what you click
  Mouse wheel .......... zoom
  Space ................ attack the nearest monster
  E .................... gather the nearest resource
  Q .................... plant a torch
  R .................... eat 2 berries to heal
  X .................... bandage Nela (stand next to her): heals her,
                         or wakes her up if she is knocked out
  C .................... crafting (at the campfire or a Crafting Table)
  B .................... build mode, then pick with 1-7:
                         1 fence, 2 reinforced wall, 3 watch post,
                         4 snap trap, 5 glow lantern, 6 storage crate,
                         7 crafting table
                         R rotate, left click place, right click / Esc cancel
  Y / F / G / T / V .... Nela: idle / follow / guard camp / gather / repair
  N .................... call the night early (daytime only)
  W A S D .............. walk directly (optional)
  H / Esc .............. help / pause

THE SCREEN (made for kids: mostly pictures, few words)
----------
  Top middle ........... sun/moon clock, one moon per night to survive
                         (they light up as nights are won), campfire
                         bar, and how many monsters are out
  Top left ............. Nela, the little sister; click a picture to give
                         a task (zzz idle, footsteps follow, shield guard,
                         basket gather, hammer repair)
  Top right ............ what you are carrying
  Bottom ............... Leo, the big brother (you): health, XP and action keys
  Small pictures pop up over Leo, e.g. a torch with a red X
  means "no torch left".
