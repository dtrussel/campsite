# Hand-play backlog

Status: ready-for-human

The checks the archived features (000&ndash;048, in `docs/history/features/`) could not tick by themselves. Each needs a person playing the game, a kid playtester, or a Windows machine with a real GPU, so every ticket is `ready-for-human`. Tick the boxes in a ticket as you go, note what you saw under `## Comments`, and set `Status: done` when it is through. Anything that needs a fix becomes a new ticket or a new `.scratch/` feature.

## Where the items came from

- **035&ndash;048:** the unticked "by hand" lines of each `test-plan.md`, grouped below.
- **016&ndash;032:** the unticked playtest, editor and Windows lines.
- **000&ndash;006:** not carried over. Their unticked boxes are the early per-phase smoke lists (WASD moves, B toggles build mode, the HUD lists 10 resources, and so on). Later work has replaced most of that behaviour, the smoke test covers the rest, and playtest 1 (044) played it all by hand.
