# Hero animation: feel and sign-off

Status: ready-for-human
Blocked by: hero-animation tickets 01–13

The hero animation feature ([spec](../../hero-animation/spec.md)) replaces Leo's and Nela's stock KayKit clips with their own motion and adds the contact frame, hit-stop, spring chains, the head look-at and face expressions. The validator and smoke test cover the numbers, but whether it *feels* better needs a person, ideally with a kid.

Play a Chapter 1 run (or more) as each hero, with the old captures from the hero-animation tickets at hand:

- [ ] Do Leo and Nela read as different kids by how they move alone, before you look at their outfits?
- [ ] Does the attack still feel instant when you press? Is the wind-up ever felt as lag, as the player's hero or when watching the companion?
- [ ] Do hits feel weightier with hit-stop and squash, without the game stuttering on a busy night with many imps?
- [ ] Do hair, packs and the lantern swing and settle naturally, never jitter, stretch or clip through the body?
- [ ] Do the feet stay planted when walking and running at every speed?
- [ ] Does the head look-at help you read who targets what, and never look broken (snapping, over-twisting)?
- [ ] At game size, can you see the blink and the hurt, happy and dazed faces? Are they worth it?
- [ ] Do idle fidgets feel lively, not distracting or too frequent?
- [ ] Is nothing uncomfortable for a young player: no camera shake, flashes or jarring motion?
- [ ] Old against new: is every clip at least as good as the stock one it replaced? Note any that should go back to stock.
