# Kid playtest questions

Status: ready-for-human
Blocked by: none

Questions to answer by watching a kid play. Best done in one session.

- [ ] Do they find the far resource spots? Do they notice the hammer cursor on damaged fences ([017](../../../docs/history/features/017-repair-and-resources/test-plan.md))?
- [ ] Do they understand that the Bramble Beast is after the fences? Is the "Bramble Beast!" banner enough ([018](../../../docs/history/features/018-bramble-beast/test-plan.md))? Is its windup readable, and is the thud and shake strong but not scary ([026](../../../docs/history/features/026-bramble-remodel/test-plan.md))?
- [ ] Do they notice the gold "full" count and connect it to crates? Do they find the Crafting Table recipes ([019](../../../docs/history/features/019-wall-crate-table/test-plan.md))?
- [ ] Do they understand what the gremlin stole? Are the banners enough? Is chasing it fun, or just annoying ([021](../../../docs/history/features/021-mushroom-gremlin/test-plan.md))? Do they see it as a sneaky goblin, can they see its ears and face from the game camera, and does the grab read ([027](../../../docs/history/features/027-gremlin-remodel/test-plan.md))?
- [ ] Is the imp cute-spooky rather than scary for young kids ([022](../../../docs/history/features/022-imp-remodel/test-plan.md))? Do they see it as a cheeky imp, and can they see the swipe coming ([028](../../../docs/history/features/028-imp-remodel/test-plan.md))?
