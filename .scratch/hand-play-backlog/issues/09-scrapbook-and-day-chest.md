# The scrapbook with a kid, and the day chest in play

Status: ready-for-human
Blocked by: none

Ranger's Journal and Camp Badges (041) is covered by the smoke test, which writes badges and pages straight into the profile and opens the day chest by moving the hero onto it. Nobody has read the scrapbook with a kid, and the day chest has never been seen in the running game: its model is the Storage Crate's chest plus a moonlit light, a stand-in until there's art ([journal and badges](../../journal-badges/spec.md)).

**The scrapbook, with a kid** (Journal on the title):

- [ ] Does the kid find the Journal button without help, and get back out (Close, or Esc)?
- [ ] On the Badges tab, can the kid read the how-to-earn lines, and do the locked silhouettes make them want to earn one? Which badge do they go after first?
- [ ] On the Journal tab, do they understand the "? ? ?" pages are still to find? Do they read Mum's pages, or skip them?
- [ ] Does a "Badge earned" toast during play get noticed, or lost in a busy night?
- [ ] Do the outfit and tent tints (and the starting perk row, once unlocked) on hero select matter to them?

**The day chest, in the running game** (a run with `day_chest_day` set, or play runs until one comes; it comes in half the runs):

- [ ] Is the chest easy to spot beside its lane at the map edge, and does the HUD banner lead the player to it?
- [ ] Does the Storage Crate model read as a treasure chest, not a crate to build or a building to repair? Does the moonlit light help?
- [ ] Is it the right size next to the heroes, sitting on the ground (not floating or sunk), and clear of trees and rocks?
- [ ] Does opening it (walking up) feel like a reward: the toast with the page title, and the page then in the Journal tab?
- [ ] Left unopened, does it fade at nightfall without looking like a bug?
