# Tool tiers: the shard trade-off

Status: ready-for-human
Blocked by: none

Tool tiers (040) are tested by the smoke test, which hands out the shards, so nobody has yet felt the choice between tool tiers and the Moon Lantern in play ([tool tiers](../../tool-tiers/spec.md)). Tiers 2 and 3 cost 2 + 4 Glow Shards per hero, and the lantern needs 11 in Chapter 1.

- [ ] Does spending shards on a tier feel like a real choice against filling the lantern, or is one side always obviously right?
- [ ] Is a tier 3 tool reachable in a normal Chapter 1 run without giving up on the lantern? Is it reachable at all for both heroes?
- [ ] Do the effects feel worth their shards: the Ember Stick's burn and light, the Blaze Stick's splash, the Double Sling's extra pebble, the Glow Sling's pierce?
- [ ] Do the tier pips under the attack slot get noticed? In the crafting panel, can you tell the tiers you already own (their button disabled) from the one you can make next?
