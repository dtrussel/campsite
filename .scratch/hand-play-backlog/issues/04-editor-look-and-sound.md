# Editor look and sound checks

Status: ready-for-human
Blocked by: none

Run the game in the editor (F5) and watch and listen.

- [ ] Listen to the day&rarr;sunset&rarr;night&rarr;dawn crossfades, the owl at sunset, chop/clink/rustle, hit, hit-stop and the imp "poof", the build thunk and craft chime, the campfire crackle, and the win and lose stingers. Then tune the volumes in `audio_library.tres`, so that nothing is harsh on laptop speakers ([016](../../../docs/history/features/016-audio-and-feel/test-plan.md)).
- [ ] Nela attacks cleanly while following, and the imps flinch while running ([023](../../../docs/history/features/023-animation-layering/test-plan.md)).
- [ ] The wind sway is gentle, not seasick ([024](../../../docs/history/features/024-living-world/test-plan.md)).
- [ ] At night, the jack-o'-lantern glow at the haunted edges reads well ([025](../../../docs/history/features/025-painted-props/test-plan.md)).
- [ ] The Bramble Beast's footstep dust lands on its footfalls ([026](../../../docs/history/features/026-bramble-remodel/test-plan.md)).
