# Ranger's Journal and Camp Badges

Status: done

From the 2026-10 brainstorm, planned as feature 041 and never started. Triaged on 2026-10-20 and split into tickets in `issues/`; the `Blocked by:` lines give the order.

## Plan

Rough size: **L**.

### Goal
Give players a reason to play again: a persistent profile that remembers achievements, tells mum's story through journal pages, and unlocks things.

### Scope
- **A profile** in `user://profile.json`, separate from the run save (`save.json` is deleted at the end of a run). It keeps:
  - badges;
  - journal pages;
  - unlocks;
  - best records (for example most nights or gremlins caught);
  - chapters completed;
  - the last hero pick (new: today `GameManager.hero_id` lives only in memory).
- **Camp Badges:** Scout-style badges for milestones, about 15 to start:
  - First Night;
  - Fire Keeper: the fire never drops below 50%;
  - Gremlin Catcher: 10 gremlins caught in total;
  - Builder: 50 buildings in total;
  - Lantern Lighter;
  - Chapter 1;
  - Chapter 2;
  - Both Heroes: win with each.
- **Journal pages:** 10–12 short pages by mum (story layer 3–4 of `story.md`). Mum's dawn notes (since 046) are collected as pages too, plus new pages. They are found as a **day chest** that appears at the map edge (one per run, 50% chance) or awarded by certain badges.
- **Scrapbook screen** from the title (a Journal button), with two tabs:
  - **Badges:** a grid with locked silhouettes and how to earn each one.
  - **Journal:** pages in a painted book frame (a placeholder frame until the team's art arrives).
- **Unlocks:**
  - Chapter 2 / 7 Nights after Chapter 1 is won;
  - cosmetic tent colours and hero outfits, as a tint only, through the existing `Stylize` tint hook;
  - starting perks that take one 039 card at the start.

  Endless mode is unlocked by the true ending itself (042), not through the profile.

### Systems touched
- New (the profile itself, and the `story.cfg` import, come from `.scratch/profile/`): `scripts/meta/badge_definition.gd` with `resources/badges/*.tres`, `resources/story/journal/*.tres`, `scripts/ui/scrapbook.gd` with `scenes/ui/Scrapbook.tscn`, and `scripts/world/day_chest.gd`.
- `scripts/core/game_manager.gd`: `stats` and `record()` feed badge checks at the end of a run and live; `run_ended` updates the profile. New stats are needed: gremlins caught, the fire's lowest HP (Fire Keeper), and whether the Moon Lantern was lit (`MoonLantern.is_full()`, Lantern Lighter).
- `scripts/ui/title_screen.gd`: the Journal button, Chapter 2 locked until unlocked, and a badge toast.
- `scripts/ui/hero_select.gd`: an outfit picker row, and the saved last pick.

### Depends on
035, 036 and 038 (done), plus **profile** (the store and the `story.cfg` import) and **run-lifecycle** (new run stats save and reset in one place). Perk cards (039) only gates the starting-perks ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `tools/check.sh` reports validate OK and smoke PASSED.
- [x] Smoke: win Chapter 1 → the profile records the chapter and the badge → the title shows Chapter 2 unlocked; this survives a restart of the game.
- [x] Smoke: a missing or corrupt profile loads as empty without errors.
- [x] Smoke: a day chest gives a page, and the page shows in the scrapbook.
- [x] Screenshots: the scrapbook's Badges and Journal tabs; the title with Chapter 2 locked and unlocked.

## Decisions so far

- **From the 2026-10 brainstorm.** The user chose story (B) and choices / meta (C), with an **adventure** tone (see `docs/design/story.md`), and asked for the roadmap first. This folder started as a plan only; it was built in tickets 01&ndash;07 (2026-10-21).
- **The profile is separate from the run save** and never deleted by a run ending. Deleting it is a Settings button that asks for confirmation.
- **Unlocks are mostly sideways** (cosmetics, story, modes) or small starting perks, so a new player isn't far behind.
- **Chapter 2 unlock for existing players** (decided 2026-10-20): when the profile is first created, Chapter 2 is unlocked if the imported `story.cfg` shows Chapter 2 was already started or finished (`intro.c2` or `ending.c2` seen; `story.cfg` never recorded a Chapter 1 win, corrected 2026-10-21), or in playtest and debug builds. New players get the lock.
- **Cosmetics are tints only** (decided 2026-10-20). Hats and capes are a follow-up once there's art.
- **`story.cfg` merges into the profile** (decided 2026-10-20), with a one-time import, so the profile is the single place progress lives. Since 2026-10-21 the store and the import are built by the profile spec; this spec adds its keys and the UI.
